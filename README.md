# Any Type Tech Solutions

### **Automation & Optimization for Industrial Problems**

We explore and develop computational solutions for complex industrial problems across **automation, optimization, modelling, and decision-making**.

Our approach is **problem-first and technology-agnostic** — evaluating whether a problem is best addressed through **Classical, AI-Assisted, Hybrid, or Quantum** methods.

---

## Motivation

Industrial systems increasingly involve complex decisions across processes, resources, designs, and interconnected operations.

The challenge is not simply to apply a new technology, but to determine:

> **What is the structure of the problem, what approaches are feasible, and what method can provide a meaningful solution?**

We work to bridge **industrial problem statements** with appropriate **computational methods**, from established techniques to emerging approaches.

---

We focus on problems involving **automation, optimization, computational modelling, and intelligent decision-making** across:

## Our Approaches

We evaluate multiple computational paradigms rather than prescribing a single technology.

| Approach        | Theoretical Foundations                                                                 | Typical Direction                                    |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Classical**   | Mathematical Optimization, Operations Research, Numerical Methods, Simulation           | Established computational solutions                  |
| **AI-Assisted** | Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Reinforcement Learning, Surrogate Modelling            | Prediction, automation & intelligent optimization    |
| **Hybrid**      | Multi-method Optimization, AI + Simulation, Classical–AI & Classical–Quantum Methods    | Combining complementary computational strengths      |
| **Quantum**     | Quantum Algorithms, Quantum Optimization, Quantum Machine Learning, Variational Methods | Exploring quantum and quantum-assisted possibilities |
